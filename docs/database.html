👤 Group A — User & Authentication
9 tables
📋 general_user_profile 📋 general_role 📋 user_role 📋 general_permission 📋 role_permission 📋 user_address 📋 user_document 📋 user_session 📋 user_otp

general_user_profile is the central user table with username, email, phone, password_hash, display_name, profile_photo_url, is_active, is_verified, and last_login_at. Supports JWT auth via user_session (access/refresh tokens, device info, IP) and OTP verification via user_otp. RBAC implemented through general_role → role_permission → general_permission chain. Users can have multiple roles via user_role (many-to-many).

🏢 Group B — Organization & Relationships
7 tables
📋 general_organization_profile 📋 org_address 📋 org_contact_person 📋 org_bank_account 📋 org_document 📋 org_user_assignment 📋 distributor_shop

general_organization_profile stores all organization types (VENDOR, DISTRIBUTOR, SHOP) with org_code, legal_name, trade_name, tax_id, org_type ENUM, and verification status. Each org can have multiple addresses (org_address with geo_fence_radius_m for GPS validation), contact persons, bank accounts, and documents. distributor_shop defines the business relationship between distributors and shops including credit_limit, payment_terms_days, and price_tier.

📦 Group C — Product & Catalog
5 tables
📋 product_category 📋 product 📋 product_image 📋 product_price_tier 📋 distributor_product

product_category supports hierarchical categories via self-referencing parent_category_id. product stores SKU, name, description, unit_of_measure, weight, dimensions, barcode, and is owned by a vendor (FK to organization). product_price_tier defines pricing levels (MRP, wholesale, distributor). distributor_product links distributors to vendor products with their own selling_price and min_order_qty.

🗃 Group D — Inventory & Warehouse
6 tables
📋 warehouse 📋 inventory 📋 inventory_batch 📋 inventory_movement 📋 goods_received_note 📋 goods_received_note_item

inventory tracks quantity_on_hand, quantity_reserved, quantity_available (computed), and reorder_level per product per warehouse. inventory_batch enables lot tracking with batch_number, manufacturing_date, expiry_date. inventory_movement logs every stock change with movement_type ENUM (STOCK_IN, STOCK_OUT, ADJUSTMENT, LOADING, RETURN, TRANSFER). goods_received_note records vendor shipments with line items.

📄 Group E — Sales Order
3 tables
📋 sales_order 📋 sales_order_item 📋 order_status_history

sales_order contains order_number, order_date, status ENUM (DRAFT through COMPLETED), subtotal, tax_total, discount_total, grand_total, and idempotency_key (UUID for offline dedup). Links to shop, distributor, and sales_rep. sales_order_item stores per-product quantities (ordered, approved, loaded, delivered, returned) with unit_price, discount, tax, and line_total. order_status_history provides full audit trail of every status transition.

🚚 Group F — Vehicle & Delivery
6 tables
📋 vehicle 📋 delivery_trip 📋 delivery_trip_order 📋 vehicle_inventory 📋 delivery_confirmation 📋 delivery_return

vehicle stores registration_number, vehicle_type, max_weight_capacity_kg, max_volume_capacity_cbm, and default_driver. delivery_trip manages trip lifecycle (PLANNED → LOADING → LOADED → IN_TRANSIT → COMPLETED). vehicle_inventory tracks quantity_loaded, quantity_delivered, quantity_returned per product per trip. delivery_confirmation captures signature_url, photo_url, GPS coordinates, and timestamp. delivery_return records returned items with reason_code ENUM.

📋 Group G — Voucher Module (Unified)
6 tables
📋 voucher_type 📋 voucher_number_sequence 📋 voucher 📋 voucher_line 📋 voucher_tax_summary 📋 voucher_attachment

Unified header+line pattern for all document types. voucher_type defines types: INV (Invoice), REC (Receipt), DN (Delivery Note), CN (Credit Note), DN_LOAD (Loading Note), GRN_V (GRN Voucher) with JasperReports template references. voucher_number_sequence manages auto-numbering with format_pattern (e.g., INV-{YEAR}-{SEQ:5}). voucher is the header with voucher_number, dates, counterparty, amounts (subtotal, tax, discount, grand_total, amount_paid, balance_due), status, and parent_voucher_id for credit notes. voucher_line stores line items. voucher_tax_summary aggregates tax by rate.

💰 Group H — Payment & Ledger
4 tables
📋 payment 📋 payment_allocation 📋 daily_collection_summary 📋 shop_ledger

payment records each collection with payment_number, amount, payment_method ENUM (CASH, CHEQUE, UPI, BANK_TRANSFER), status ENUM (COLLECTED → VERIFIED → DEPOSITED → CLEARED/BOUNCED), cheque details, and GPS coordinates. payment_allocation links payments to specific invoices (many-to-many). daily_collection_summary tracks total_cash_collected, total_cheques, total_digital, cash_handed_over with variance detection. shop_ledger maintains running_balance per shop with DEBIT/CREDIT entries.

📍 Group I — Route & Field Operations
4 tables
📋 route_plan 📋 route_plan_stop 📋 shop_visit_photo 📋 gps_tracking_log

route_plan assigns a sales rep to a date with planned shops. route_plan_stop defines each shop visit with visit_sequence, planned vs actual check-in/check-out times, and visit_status. shop_visit_photo stores photos captured during visits. gps_tracking_log records periodic location updates (latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, battery_level) for both sales reps and delivery vehicles.

⚙ Group J — Tax & Configuration
3 tables
📋 tax_rate 📋 system_configuration 📋 currency

tax_rate defines configurable tax rules per name, rate percentage, and applicable region/category. system_configuration is a key-value store for feature flags, business rules, and system settings. currency stores currency codes, symbols, and exchange rates for multi-currency support.

📜 Group K — Notification & Audit
4 tables
📋 notification 📋 notification_preference 📋 audit_log 📋 sync_log

notification stores all notifications with type, channel (PUSH, SMS, EMAIL, WHATSAPP), title, body, is_read, and related entity references. notification_preference lets users configure which channels they want per event type. audit_log records all sensitive operations (entity_type, entity_id, action, old_value, new_value, performed_by, IP address). sync_log tracks mobile offline sync operations with conflict detection.
