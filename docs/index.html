Core Functions

What the System Does

10 major functional areas covering the complete distribution lifecycle

📦

F1 — Multi-Vendor Catalog Management

Vendors manage product catalogs with SKUs, images, pricing tiers. Distributors select which products to carry and set their own selling prices.

🏢

F2 — Distributor Onboarding & Management

Distributors register, manage warehouses, vehicles, staff, and configure business rules like credit limits and payment terms.

📍

F3 — Sales Rep Field Operations

Route planning, shop visits with GPS check-in, order collection (offline-capable), payment collection, and daily summaries.

📄

F4 — Order Management

Full order lifecycle: Draft → Submitted → Approved → Processing → Loaded → In Transit → Delivered → Completed.

🗃

F5 — Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking across warehouses and vehicles. GRN, batch tracking, stock adjustments, low-stock alerts, and movement history.

🚚

F6 — Vehicle & Delivery Management

Load planning with capacity validation, route optimization, real-time GPS tracking, delivery confirmation with signature and photo proof.

📋

F7 — Voucher Module (DN, Invoice, Receipt, CN)

Unified voucher system for Delivery Notes, Invoices, Receipts, Credit Notes, and Loading Notes. Auto-generated PDFs via JasperReports.

💰

F8 — Payment Collection & Reconciliation

Multi-mode payments (Cash, Cheque, UPI, Bank Transfer, Credit). Daily collection summaries, verification workflow, and shop ledger.

🔔

F9 — Notifications & Alerts

Push notifications (FCM), SMS, Email (Jakarta Mail), and WhatsApp (Meta API) for order updates, delivery, payments, and low stock alerts.

📊

F10 — Reporting & Analytics

Sales dashboards, rep performance, product analytics, inventory reports, financial summaries, and receivables aging. Export to PDF/Excel.

Key Highlights

Why This Architecture

🔌

Offline-First Mobile

All field operations work offline with SQLite local DB. Data syncs automatically when connectivity is restored. Idempotency keys prevent duplicates.

📚

Unified Voucher Module

Single header+line pattern handles Invoices, Receipts, Delivery Notes, Credit Notes, and Loading Notes. Auto-numbered, PDF-ready via JasperReports.

🔒

Enterprise-Grade Security

JWT authentication, BCrypt password hashing, RBAC with granular permissions, TLS 1.3, PCI-DSS compliance, and comprehensive audit logging.

🚀

Monolithic Simplicity

Single WAR deployment on WildFly 31. Simpler DevOps, easier debugging, lower infrastructure cost. Docker Compose for all services.

📈

Real-Time Analytics

JasperReports for PDF/Excel generation. Recharts dashboards. Sales, inventory, delivery, and financial reports with date range filters.

🌎

Multi-Channel Notifications

Push (FCM/APNs), Email (Jakarta Mail), SMS, and WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API). Configurable per user and event type.
